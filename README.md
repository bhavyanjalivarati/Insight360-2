# 🚀 Insight360
Customer Intelligence & Customer 360 Platform

Insight360 is a platform that puts all customer information in one place. It helps sales teams understand customers, find problems early, improve relationships, and increase customer retention.

💡 Problem

Customer information is often spread across different systems like sales, payments, support, and customer interactions.

Insight360 brings this information together so the team can easily understand the complete customer situation.

⭐ Main Features
👤 Customer 360 – View complete customer details in one place.
❤️ Health Score – See how healthy the customer relationship is.
⚠️ Churn Risk – Identify customers who may leave.
🤝 Relationship Tracking – Find customers whose relationships need attention.
🔄 Recovery Actions – Help sales teams improve weak customer relationships.
🏆 Loyalty Levels – Track customer loyalty and benefits.
📊 Sales Pipeline – Track leads, deals, and sales progress.
📋 Customer Submission – Collect and review new customer information.
💳 Payment Tracking – Monitor customer payment status.
📈 Analytics – View important customer and sales information through dashboards.
🔐 Role-Based Access – Different access for Admin, Manager, Sales Executive, and Customer.

🚀 Insight360
Customer Intelligence & Customer 360 Platform

Insight360 is a customer intelligence platform that brings customer information, sales data, payments, relationships, loyalty, and customer health into one place. It helps businesses understand their customers better and take timely actions to improve customer relationships and growth.

💡 Problem

In many businesses, customer information is stored in different places such as CRM systems, sales records, payment records, and support activities. This makes it difficult for sales teams to get a complete picture of a customer.

Insight360 solves this by bringing important customer information into one unified Customer 360 view.

⭐ Key Features
👤 Customer 360

Provides a complete view of each customer, including their profile, sales activities, deals, payments, relationships, and support information.

❤️ Customer Health Score

Shows the overall health of the customer relationship and helps the team identify customers who may need attention.

⚠️ Churn Risk

Helps identify customers who show signs of reduced engagement or other potential risks, allowing the sales team to investigate and take action.

🤝 Relationship & Recovery

Tracks customer relationships and highlights areas where engagement may be weakening. Sales teams can then take recovery actions such as follow-ups or resolving pending issues.

🏆 Loyalty Management

Groups customers into loyalty levels and helps businesses understand their valuable customers and provide suitable benefits.

📊 Sales Pipeline

Allows managers and sales executives to track leads, opportunities, deals, sales stages, and progress.

📋 Customer Submission

Provides a form for collecting new customer information. The submission first goes to the manager for review. After approval and assignment to a sales executive, it can be imported as an actual customer.

💳 Payment Tracking

Tracks customer payment information and statuses such as paid, pending, partially paid, or overdue.

📈 Analytics Dashboard

Displays customer and sales information through dashboards and charts, making important patterns easier to understand.

🔐 Role-Based Access

Provides different access levels for Admin, Manager, Sales Executive, and Customer, keeping responsibilities and information organized.

👥 User Roles

Admin – Manages the overall platform and users.

Manager – Reviews customer requests, manages sales executives, and monitors customers and team performance.

Sales Executive – Handles assigned customers, deals, follow-ups, and customer activities.

Customer – Provides required information and accesses relevant customer features.

🛠️ Technology Stack

Frontend: React, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind CSS

Backend: Node.js, Express, TypeScript

The goal of Insight360 is to turn scattered customer information into a simple 360° view, helping businesses understand customer needs, identify risks, improve relationships, increase loyalty, and support better sales decisions.
Database: SQLite / SQL.js and MongoDB

Security: JWT authentication, bcrypt password hashing, and role-based access control.
